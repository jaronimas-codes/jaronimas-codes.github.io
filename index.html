01 — About

My work sits at the intersection of data engineering, analytics, and practical software development. The goal isn't just to analyze data — it's to make it usable, scalable, and operational.

I currently oversee and maintain a dynamic product portfolio of 9+ million items, developing Flask-based applications for data quality control, and contributing to tools that extract and transform data from PostgreSQL databases into various formats for seamless integration.

My approach is pragmatic: clean the data, structure it properly, build systems others can reliably use. I look for bottlenecks, repetitive tasks, and fragile processes — and replace them with stable, scalable solutions.

I hold a Master of Science in Data Science and Applied Information from Lucerne University of Applied Sciences and Arts (graduated 2025) and a Bachelor of Business Administration and Management from Vilnius University (graduated 2011). Languages: Lithuanian (Native), English (C1), German (B1 aiming C1).

Outside of work, I'm an active member of the World Organization of the Scout Movement – Lietuvos Skautija since 1996, involved in outdoor education, leadership, and community service. Personal interests include regular physical activity, snowboarding, hiking, mountain biking, trail running, mushroom hunting, and playing guitar and bass guitar.
